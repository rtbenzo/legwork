Support

Legwork plans a route of up to 40 stops, then opens the next stop in Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.

  • Add stops by search, a pasted list, a CSV file, voice, a photo of a label, a contact, or a pin.
  • Set a start, a return to start, or a fixed end. Add time windows, priority stops, service minutes, and a break.
  • On the route, mark a stop delivered, skipped, or failed. Save a photo or signature on the stop.
  • Load order lists the pending stops in reverse so the first delivery is at the door.
  • Routes stay on this iPhone. Delete the app to remove them.
