Legwork · NetCore LLC

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Legwork (App Store name Legwork: Route Optimizer) is published by NetCore LLC. It plans a driving route from stops you add. It does not require an account. NetCore LLC does not run a server that receives your routes.

Who this applies to

The iOS app Legwork (bundle ID com.netcore.legwork).

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores the route on this device:

  • Stop addresses, recipient names, phone numbers, package notes, and time windows
  • Photos and signatures you save as proof of a stop
  • The maps app, service time, and break settings you choose

Deleting the app deletes that local store. A device backup, such as iCloud Backup or a Finder backup, may include the app container. That backup is Apple’s, not a NetCore LLC database.

Location

If you allow location, Legwork uses it as the start of a route and to order stops by drive time. The fix is used on the device and is also sent to Apple with the direction request described below. NetCore LLC does not receive your location.

Addresses and drive times

Looking up an address and calculating drive time uses Apple’s maps services on the device. Apple receives the addresses and coordinates needed to answer that request. NetCore LLC does not receive them.

Navigation

When you open the next stop, that stop is handed to the app you picked: Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze. From that moment, that company’s own policy applies. Legwork does not receive your turn-by-turn trip.

Voice

If you add a stop by voice, the microphone records what you say so it can be turned into text. That audio is processed by Apple’s speech recognition. NetCore LLC does not receive the recording or the transcript.

Camera, photos, and contacts

A photo of a label is read on this iPhone to fill in an address. It is not uploaded to NetCore LLC. A contact is used only when you pick one. The name, address, and phone number you pick are copied onto the route stored on this iPhone.

Phone calls

Calling a recipient opens the Phone app. Legwork does not place the call itself and does not receive the call.

What NetCore LLC does not collect

No account, name, email, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, or tracking across apps and websites. No ads. NetCore LLC does not sell information because it does not receive your route.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.
